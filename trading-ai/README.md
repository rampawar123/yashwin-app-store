# YASHWIN AI Trading Assistant

Personal AI-powered market analysis and trading automation project.

## Phase 1

- Professional UI/UX foundation
- Market analysis dashboard
- Paper trading
- Strategy engine foundation
- Risk management foundation
- No real-money trading

## Planned

- Live market data
- Web and news research
- AI market analysis
- Option-chain analysis
- Strategy engine
- Risk engine
- Paper trading
- Angel One SmartAPI
- Position manager
- Automatic Stop Loss and Target
- Alerts
- Trade history
- 24x7 server

## Safety

This system does not guarantee profit.

Real-money trading will only be enabled after extensive testing,
paper trading, validation and risk-control verification.

## Trading Modes

1. Paper Trading
2. Semi-Automatic Trading
3. Live Trading

Paper Trading is the default mode during development.

## Project Principle

The system must have strong risk controls before any live
order-execution capability is enabled.

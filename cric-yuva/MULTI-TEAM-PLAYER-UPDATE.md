# CRIC YUVA — Multi-Team & Multi-Player Update

## Team management
- Multiple teams can now be created and saved independently.
- Creating a new team never overwrites the currently selected team.
- `MY TEAMS` switcher appears on the My Team screen.
- Each team keeps its own player roster.
- Active team is remembered and is used by Start Match.
- Duplicate team names are blocked.

## Player management
- `ADD PLAYER` opens the direct Name/Profile form.
- Player ID is not required from the user.
- Multiple players can be added to the same team.
- `SAVE & ADD ANOTHER` keeps the form open for fast squad entry.
- Duplicate player names are blocked within the same team.
- The same player name can still exist in different teams.
- Existing registered-player search remains available through `SEARCH PLAYER`.

## Match flow
- Saved teams remain available to Team A / Team B selection.
- Playing XI continues to load the roster belonging to the selected team.
- Existing scoring, tournament, stats, history and cloud/local persistence are preserved.
